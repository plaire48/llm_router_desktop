# Result and visual hierarchy — 2026-10-12

## User goal

Prioritize solving the user's request. Make conversation, planning, execution and results readable; use color and emphasis consistently across Workspace screens, components and portal dialogs. Preserve all essential information and controls at narrow widths and large text sizes. Remove the token number beside the composer donut.

## Decisions and application

- Retain existing CX components, Pretendard typography and Nubi palette. No new UI dependency or copied third-party product skin. Semantic aliases in `workspace-identity.css` pair foreground and background for action, success, warning and error. Primary, secondary and metadata have separate contrast; labels use 500 and key decisions use 600. Provider brand colors remain intact.
- Apply the hierarchy through shared status, field, guidance and disclosure primitives and the common dialog contract. Reuse meaningful icons and retain text/accessibility labels. Do not recolor every icon blue or make entire paragraphs bold.
- Keep current activity and its elapsed time visible; detailed plans and records remain expandable. Result review now displays unresolved requirements and their concrete missing evidence outside disclosure. Next actions are distinct; internal review rounds remain inside details. An omitted unresolved child report remains visible. These indicators describe observed review state, not a guarantee of task completion.
- Strengthen the actual actor and independent reviewer instructions: address the requested outcome, inspect execution results, continue feasible authorized work, and state verification limits. Do not stop at a plan, activity log or generic completion claim. Do not request cosmetic retries when the solution is adequate. Existing host receipts, requirement checks, approval boundaries and bounded recovery remain unchanged.
- Constrain dialogs to the viewport, preserve header/footer and let the body scroll without shrinking its child content. Fields can shrink within their layout. Title labels keep full tooltips where generated by the shared Desktop title helper.
- Composer donut shows its internal ratio only. Exact/estimated tokens remain in the details dialog; token accounting is unchanged.
